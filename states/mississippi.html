Apprenticeships in Mississippi

Who runs it: Mississippi Apprenticeship Program (MAP), Mississippi Department of Employment Security; programs are registered by the U.S. DOL Office of Apprenticeship. The U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Find openings on the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder and apply directly to the employer or sponsor.
  2. Generally you must be at least 16, or 18 for hazardous occupations. MAP helps employers set up programs and does not hire apprentices.
  3. Contact: apprenticesinfo@mdes.ms.gov, 601-321-6000.
  4. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
