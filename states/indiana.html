Apprenticeships in Indiana

Who runs it: Indiana Department of Workforce Development, Office of Work-Based Learning and Apprenticeship (OWBLA); programs are registered by the U.S. DOL Office of Apprenticeship in Indiana. The U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Find an employer-sponsored registered apprenticeship, or a pre-apprenticeship, in your occupation.
  2. Apply through the employer, union or your local WorkOne Career Center. Apprenticeship programs are employer-driven, so you have to be hired.
  3. Once accepted, review the program standards and sign the apprenticeship agreement. Apprenticeship Indiana lists industries from advanced manufacturing to IT.
  4. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
