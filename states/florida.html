Apprenticeships in Florida

Who runs it: The Florida Department of Education's Office of Apprenticeship is the official state apprenticeship agency, authorized by the U.S. Department of Labor. It oversees registered apprenticeship and pre-apprenticeship programs. Apprentice Florida, a partnership of FDOE, FloridaCommerce and CareerSource Florida, promotes apprenticeship.

How US apprenticeship works

Unlike in Canada, you don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

Eligibility

Set by each program sponsor, so check the program's requirements before applying.

How to find one

  1. Use FDOE's Apprenticeship Finder Map, the statewide sponsor contact lists (Excel) on the FDOE contacts page, or apprenticeship.gov's Apprenticeship Finder.
  2. Apply directly to the sponsor or employer. Some programs may not be taking new applicants.
  3. For help, contact the Apprenticeship Training Representative for your region (FDOE lists 9 regions) or a CareerSource Florida career center.
