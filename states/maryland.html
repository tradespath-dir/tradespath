Apprenticeships in Maryland

Who runs it: The Office of Apprenticeship, part of the Maryland Department of Labor's Division of Workforce Development and Adult Learning, the state apprenticeship agency. The office was created by the RAISE Act of 2025.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to apply

  1. Search the Apprenticeship Locator by county, occupation, industry or sponsor. It lists Registered Apprenticeship programs that expect to have openings for new apprentices.
  2. Listed programs may not be hiring right now. Contact the program directly about openings, deadlines and how to apply.
  3. Apprenticeships are jobs from day one. Most require an application and/or interview, and you must be eligible to work in the US. Most need little or no prior experience, but qualifications vary.
  4. For in-person help, go to an American Job Center or use the Maryland Workforce Exchange. Office of Apprenticeship: 410-767-2246.
