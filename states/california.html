Apprenticeships in California

Who runs it: The Division of Apprenticeship Standards (DAS), part of the California Department of Industrial Relations, is the state agency that approves and registers apprenticeship programs.

How US apprenticeship works

Unlike in Canada, you don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

Eligibility

Set by each program. Some prefer a high school diploma or equivalent, some have stricter requirements, and some have none. Many require an aptitude or other test, and most require English proficiency. Each program's listing gives its minimum age, education, physical and other requirements (for example, a driver's licence).

How to find one

  1. Search DAS's registered program database by county and occupation.
  2. Being registered doesn't mean a program is accepting applications. Each program sets its own recruiting schedule, so contact it to find out when it opens.
  3. Apply in the way the listing describes.
  4. Not ready yet? A pre-apprenticeship program can prepare you for entry tests.
