Apprenticeships in Delaware

Who runs it: Delaware Department of Labor, Apprenticeship and Training (the State Apprenticeship Agency). A State Apprenticeship Agency registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Delaware lists 500+ registered programs, 2,100+ registered apprentices and 40+ occupations with programs.
  2. Find a sponsor in your trade on the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder and apply directly. Apprentices are paid from their first day of work.
  3. For pre-apprenticeship programs that can prepare you, contact the Apprenticeship Office: 302-761-8328, apprenticeship@delaware.gov.
  4. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
