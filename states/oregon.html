Apprenticeships in Oregon

Who runs it: The Apprenticeship and Training Division (ATD) of the Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries (BOLI) registers and supports apprenticeship programs.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Browse BOLI's "Find an apprenticeship program" listings by trade or career, and apply to the program you choose.
  2. Not ready yet? BOLI lists certified pre-apprenticeship programs.
  3. Once registered, use the Apprentice Resource Directory. Veterans can get dedicated career counseling and advocacy.
  4. Help: 971-245-3844 or ATD.General@boli.oregon.gov.

Money help

Registered construction apprentices with young children may qualify for Child Care for Construction, which caps child-care costs at 7% of household income.
