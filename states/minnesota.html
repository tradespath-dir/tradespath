Apprenticeships in Minnesota

Who runs it: Apprenticeship Minnesota, part of the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry (DLI). Each apprenticeship is a written agreement between the apprentice and the employer, registered with the state. It sets out the length of training, school hours, skills to learn and wages.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

Eligibility

  • Generally, a high school diploma or GED. Math, science and industrial or technical courses help.
  • DLI says Minnesota residents over 18 with a diploma or GED are eligible to apply. Prior college is welcome but not required.
  • There are 100+ apprenticeable occupations in Minnesota. Hiring is competitive and depends on available jobs.

How to apply

  1. Choose a trade.
  2. Use DLI's Registered Apprenticeship Program Application Directory (the largest and most-requested programs) or the USDOL Partner Finder (all programs).
  3. Contact the program sponsor directly. Each program has its own process and schedule.
  4. Questions: Apprenticeship Minnesota, 651-284-5090 or 1-800-342-5354, dli.apprenticeship@state.mn.us.
