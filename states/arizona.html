Apprenticeships in Arizona

Who runs it: The Arizona Apprenticeship Office at the Arizona Department of Economic Security (DES). Programs must be registered with it.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

Key facts

  • Minimum age is 16, but each industry sets its own and it is usually 18. There is no upper age limit.
  • No experience is needed. Most programs require a high school diploma or GED, and some need courses such as algebra or blueprint reading.
  • Apprentices are full-time employees (part-time is allowed in School-to-Registered Apprenticeship). Pay typically starts at 40 to 50% of a skilled worker's wage and rises as you progress.
  • Most programs require about 144 hours of Related Technical Instruction a year.
  • Programs last 1 to 6 years, most 3 to 4.

How to apply

  1. Check the DES list of active Registered Apprenticeship programs.
  2. Pick the occupation you want.
  3. Contact the program representative directly. Ask whether and when they take applications and which documents you need.
  4. Questions: AZApprenticeship@azdes.gov.
