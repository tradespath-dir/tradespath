Apprenticeships in Pennsylvania

Who runs it: The Apprenticeship & Training Office (ATO) at the Pennsylvania Department of Labor & Industry acts as the State Apprenticeship Agency. It approves and monitors Registered Apprenticeship (RA) and pre-apprenticeship programs.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

What you get

  • A paid job from day one.
  • Structured on-the-job learning with a mentor.
  • Classroom learning (virtual or in person).
  • A portable, nationally recognized credential when you finish.

How to start

  1. Search open programs on the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder, or ask at a PA CareerLink® office.
  2. Apply to the employer or sponsor.
  3. Questions: the ATO answers at apprenticeship@pa.gov or (717) 787-6997.
