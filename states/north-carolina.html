Apprenticeships in North Carolina

Who runs it: ApprenticeshipNC, North Carolina's State Apprenticeship Agency, part of the NC Community College System. It helps employers build Registered Apprenticeship programs.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

What you get

  • A paycheck from day one, with structured wage increases as you progress.
  • Hands-on training plus classroom instruction.
  • A nationally recognized credential.
  • Open to high school students, career changers and military veterans.

How to start

  1. Search the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder for North Carolina openings, or ask your local community college.
  2. Apply to the employer or sponsor.
  3. Questions: ApprenticeshipNC, 919-807-6988, apprenticeshipnc@nccommunitycolleges.edu.
