Apprenticeships in Massachusetts

Who runs it: The Division of Apprentice Standards (DAS) registers apprenticeship programs and each apprentice's agreement.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

What state law requires (M.G.L. c. 23, s. 11I)

  • At least 2,000 hours of employment as an apprentice.
  • At least 150 hours a year of related classroom instruction (or what the licensing body requires for licensed trades).
  • A schedule of the work processes you will learn.
  • A progressively increasing wage scale.
  • Your apprentice agreement must be registered with DAS within 30 days of signing.

How to start

  1. Read DAS's "Become an apprentice in Massachusetts" page.
  2. Find openings on the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder and apply to the employer or sponsor.
  3. Once hired, your sponsor files your agreement with DAS.
