Apprenticeships in Tennessee

Who runs it: Apprenticeship TN, the state's apprenticeship office. Programs in its directory are registered with the U.S. Department of Labor.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to apply

  1. Open Apprenticeship TN's "Find a Program" directory. It lists about 1,750 program entries (as of September 30, 2026) and can be sorted by program name or county.
  2. Contact the program you want directly to apply.
  3. Also search the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder. For more help, visit an American Job Center.
  4. Expect program-specific steps. Some trade programs use aptitude tests, ranked eligibility lists and interviews.
  5. Questions: Apprenticeship.TN@tn.gov.
