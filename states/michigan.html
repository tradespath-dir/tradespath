Apprenticeships in Michigan

Who runs it: In Michigan, Registered Apprenticeship programs are registered and overseen by the U.S. Department of Labor's Michigan Office of Apprenticeship. The state Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity (LEO) promotes and expands apprenticeship through its State Apprenticeship Expansion (SAE) team.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

What you get

  • A paid job with wage increases as your skills grow, and a mentor.
  • Classroom instruction, which can be at a high school, college, union training centre, employer site or online.
  • A nationally recognized credential when you finish.

How to start

  1. Search openings on Pure Michigan Talent Connect (the state job board) or the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.
  2. Get free guidance from your local Michigan Works! office.
  3. Not ready yet? Look at a MARC-endorsed pre-apprenticeship program.
  4. Some programs require an entrance test such as ACT WorkKeys, so check each program's requirements.
  5. Questions: LEO-Apprenticeship@michigan.gov, 517-335-5858.
