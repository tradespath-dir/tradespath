Apprenticeships in North Dakota

Who runs it: Job Service North Dakota and ND Commerce; programs are registered by the U.S. DOL Office of Apprenticeship. The U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Search open apprenticeship jobs through Job Service North Dakota (ND Workforce Connection) or the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.
  2. Apply directly to the employer or program. Contact your local Job Service office for referrals.
  3. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
