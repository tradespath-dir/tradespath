Apprenticeships in Colorado

Who runs it: Apprenticeship Colorado, the Colorado State Apprenticeship Agency within the Colorado Department of Labor and Employment (CDLE). A State Apprenticeship Agency registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Browse the Colorado Registered Apprenticeship Program Directory, which lists every program registered in Colorado with its length, qualifications and selection criteria. It is not a job board.
  2. Find current openings on Connecting Colorado or the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder. Confirm the program appears in the Colorado directory.
  3. Apply directly to the sponsor or employer. Apprenticeship Colorado does not place applicants.
  4. Prepare for prerequisites (for example a driver's license or GED) and the program's selection steps (application, placement test, hiring fair). There is no residency requirement, but each sponsor decides on out-of-state applicants.
  5. Contact: apprenticeship@state.co.us, (720) 204-8608.
  6. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
