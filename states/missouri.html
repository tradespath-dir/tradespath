Apprenticeships in Missouri

Who runs it: Missouri Office of Apprenticeship (Department of Higher Education and Workforce Development); programs are registered by the U.S. DOL Office of Apprenticeship. The U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. Browse JobsMoGov's Missouri Registered Apprenticeships listings by occupation, location or sponsor.
  2. Search current openings on Missouri Apprentice Connect (create an apprentice account) or the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.
  3. Apply through the posting or directly with the sponsor.
  4. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
