Apprenticeships in New Jersey

Who runs it: New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Office of Apprenticeship (New Jersey Apprenticeship Network); programs are registered by the U.S. DOL Office of Apprenticeship. The U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship registers programs here.

How US apprenticeship works

You don't register with the government yourself. You apply to an employer or program sponsor, and the sponsor registers you once you're accepted. Registered apprentices are paid employees who get on-the-job training plus classroom instruction and earn a nationally recognized credential.

How to start

  1. The Office of Apprenticeship is the state's main contact for current and future apprentices in New Jersey Apprenticeship Network (NJAN) initiatives.
  2. Find a registered program on the Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder and apply directly to the employer or sponsor. Apprenticeships go beyond construction.
  3. Visit any One-Stop Career Center for in-person help.
  4. Search openings anywhere in the US: Apprenticeship.gov Apprenticeship Finder.

Money help

Some apprentices taking college-credit related instruction may qualify for federal aid: see Paying for trade school (Pell Grants). Ask your local American Job Center about WIOA training funds.
