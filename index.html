How apprenticeship works (short version)

You get hired by an employer as an apprentice, register with your province or territory, and earn a wage while you learn. Most of your training happens on the job; the rest is technical (classroom) training, usually in blocks. After the required hours and exams you earn your provincial or territorial certificate, and in many trades the Red Seal endorsement lets you work across Canada.
