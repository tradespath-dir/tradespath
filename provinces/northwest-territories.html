Apprenticeships in the Northwest Territories

Who runs it: The Apprenticeship, Trade and Occupation Certification (ATOC) Program, run by the Department of Education, Culture and Employment (ECE), Government of the NWT. It covers 43 designated trades and 17 occupations.

For high school students: SNAP

The Schools North Apprenticeship Program (SNAP) is a work experience program. Students earn wages and high school credits, plus work experience hours toward a trades credential.

  • At least 16, attending high school in the NWT, with a SIN.
  • Parent/guardian consent if under 18.
  • Keep up attendance and schoolwork, and get an agreement from an employer.

Ask your school or regional ECE Service Centre.

How to start an apprenticeship

  1. Find an employer in a designated trade willing to train you.
  2. Contact your regional ECE Service Centre. Staff there register apprentices and monitor workplace training.
  3. Combine on-the-job hours with technical training and exams.
