Apprenticeships in Ontario

Who runs it: Skilled Trades Ontario (STO) registers apprentices and training agreements, sets training standards and runs certification exams. The Ministry of Labour, Immigration, Training and Skills Development runs programs like OYAP and supports through Employment Ontario, which can help match you with a sponsor.

For high school students: OYAP

  • Ontario Youth Apprenticeship Program: be at least 15, in high school (Grade 11 or 12 or equivalent), with at least 14 credits. Some schools allow it the summer after Grade 10.
  • OYAP-FAST is an accelerated stream giving more apprenticeship learning while earning your diploma, with a seal on the OSSD.
  • Apply through your school board's OYAP recruiter, guidance counsellor or co-op teacher.

How to start an apprenticeship (Skilled Trades Ontario)

  1. Qualify: at least 16, legally allowed to work in Canada (e.g. SIN), meet the trade's education requirements.
  2. Find a sponsor (employer, union or group sponsor) registered with STO. Employment Ontario, college career centres, unions and school programs can help.
  3. Apply: 18+ online through the STO Portal; 16 to 17 by paper application signed by a parent/guardian and your sponsor.
  4. Once approved, sign your Registered Training Agreement. You get a wallet card and a training logbook.
