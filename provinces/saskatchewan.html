Apprenticeships in Saskatchewan

Who runs it: The Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Commission (SATCC). There are more than 40 designated trades.

How apprenticeship works here

  • Apprentices spend about 85% of their time on the job learning from a journeyperson, and attend technical training once a year for 6 to 10 weeks, depending on the trade.
  • Most apprenticeships take two to four years.
  • Apprenticeship is mandatory in five compulsory trades: Construction Electrician, Plumber, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning Mechanic, Sheet Metal Worker, and Sprinkler Fitter.

For high school students: Saskatchewan Youth Apprenticeship (SYA)

SYA is a set of 12 challenges high school students complete to prepare for apprenticeship. If you complete SYA and register as an apprentice in Saskatchewan within five years, you get a waived registration fee, no charge for Level 1 technical training, and 300 hours of trade-time credit. SATCC also awards $1,000 SYA Industry Scholarships (110 awarded for 2025-26). Ask your school whether it runs SYA.

Entrance requirements

A completed Grade 12 transcript, CAEC or GED guarantees entry, but "modified" or "general" math and science classes don't meet requirements. Grade 12 is encouraged but not required; each trade has minimum math and science requirements.

How to start

  1. Find an employer willing to hire and train you.
  2. Apply online through MyATC.
  3. Complete your training levels, then write the journeyperson certification exam (and practical exam if required).
