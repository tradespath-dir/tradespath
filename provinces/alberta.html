Apprenticeships in Alberta

Who runs it: Apprenticeship and Industry Training (AIT), Government of Alberta, under the Skilled Trades and Apprenticeship Education Act. Information, applications and apprentice accounts are on Tradesecrets / MyTradesecrets. The Alberta Board of Skilled Trades advises on the system.

For high school students: Registered Apprenticeship Program (RAP)

  • Open to senior high school students aged 15+, with informed consent from a parent or guardian.
  • Required prerequisite course: HCS3000 Workplace Safety Systems.
  • You need an employer in your trade who will take you on, have you supervised by a qualified journeyperson, and pay at least minimum wage.
  • Your school's off-campus teacher approves the worksite; you then apply online (free) to AIT and receive your Record Book.
  • You earn 1 high school credit per 25 hours on the job. Each RAP course (15, 25, 35) is 125 hours and 5 credits.

Start with the RAP or off-campus coordinator at your high school. AIT information line: 1-800-248-4823.

After high school

  1. Confirm your trade is a designated trade in Alberta (Trade Profiles on Tradesecrets).
  2. Find an employer (sponsor) willing to train you.
  3. Apply for an apprenticeship through Tradesecrets.
  4. Combine on-the-job learning with classroom instruction at a post-secondary institution (typically 6 to 12 weeks per year, depending on the trade), and pass the exam each period.
