Apprenticeships in Nova Scotia

Who runs it: The Nova Scotia Apprenticeship Agency (NSAA).

How Canadian apprenticeship works

In most provinces you first need a job with an employer in the trade. Then you and the employer register you with the provincial apprenticeship authority. You alternate paid on-the-job hours with technical training blocks, and many trades lead to a Red Seal endorsement.

How to start

  1. Get a job in the trade with an employer who can provide supervision by a certified journeyperson. You must be employed to register.
  2. Ask for an Industry Training Consultant. In compulsory trades you need a temporary permit to start working.
  3. You and your employer sign an apprenticeship agreement. The application package goes to the NSAA, and processing takes about 10 to 15 business days.
  4. You can ask for credit for prior experience. High school students aged 16 to 19 can register as Youth Apprentices.
  5. Contact: 902-424-5651 or 1-800-494-5651.
