Apprenticeships in Nunavut

Who runs it: The Apprenticeship Unit of the Department of Family Services, Government of Nunavut, with the Nunavut Apprenticeship, Trade and Occupations Certification Board. Regional Career Development Officers (CDOs) help with registration.

Eligibility

  • At least 16 years old.
  • An employer willing to take you on as an apprentice, with a certified journeyperson in your trade at the same work location. A CDO can help match you with an employer.
  • Pass the Trades Entrance Exam (tutoring available), or complete the Skilled Trade Workers Foundations block at Nunavut Arctic College in Rankin Inlet.
  • You must be a Nunavut resident to receive Family Services funding.

How it works

  • Paid on-the-job training under a certified journeyperson, plus 8 to 12 weeks of in-class technical training, in Rankin Inlet or southern Canada.
  • Completion usually takes 2 to 4 years. Certification is recognized territorially and nationally (Red Seal where applicable).
  • Employers must pay you according to the regulations, release you for training, and submit your Record of Employment so you can apply for EI during training.

How to apply

  1. Contact the Career Development Office in your region (Kivalliq, Kitikmeot, Qikiqtaaluk/Baffin).
  2. You and your employer complete the Nunavut Apprentice Application and sign the apprenticeship contract.
