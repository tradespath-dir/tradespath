Apprenticeships in Manitoba

Who runs it: Apprenticeship Manitoba (Province of Manitoba), with the Apprenticeship and Certification Board. Since January 15, 2025, apprenticeship applications are accepted only online through AccessManitoba.

For high school students: High School Apprenticeship Program (HSAP)

  • For students in Grades 10, 11 or 12, aged 16 (15 in Grade 10 with a Young Worker Readiness Certificate). Parent/guardian consent if under 18.
  • You find a qualified, insured employer in a designated trade, then complete an Apprenticeship Application and Agreement through your school's HSAP educator.
  • Pay is at least minimum wage plus 10%. You can earn up to 8 high school credits (1 credit per 110 hours on the job).
  • After graduating, your hours can transfer into a post-secondary apprenticeship, and eligible student-apprentices can have technical training fees covered for up to four levels (one level per 220 hours, max 880).
  • Mature students at Adult Learning Centres can earn up to 6 credits.

How apprenticeship works here

Roughly 80% of training is on the job and 20% in-school technical training. Most trades take two to four years.

How to start after high school

  1. Choose a designated trade and find an employer willing to train you.
  2. Apply online through AccessManitoba.
  3. Log on-the-job hours and register for technical training levels.
