Apprenticeships in British Columbia

Who runs it: SkilledTradesBC, a provincial Crown corporation that manages apprenticeship, trades certification and youth trades programs.

For high school students: Youth trades programs

  • Youth Discover the Trades: introduces what a trade is with hands-on activities.
  • Youth Explore the Trades: try different trades and build skills.
  • Youth Train in Trades: trades training in a specific trade while still in high school.
  • Youth Work in Trades: paid on-the-job training while attending high school.

Ask your school or district career office which programs it offers.

Foundation (pre-apprenticeship) programs

Foundation programs at training providers give entry-level trades training before you have an employer. SkilledTradesBC reports nearly half of foundation students continue into an apprenticeship.

How to start an apprenticeship

  1. Find an employer (sponsor) in your trade.
  2. Register as an apprentice with SkilledTradesBC.
  3. Combine work-based hours with technical training levels and exams.

Note: BC is moving a number of trades to Skilled Trades Certification (compulsory trades). In those trades you must be certified or a registered apprentice to do the work. Check SkilledTradesBC for the current list.
