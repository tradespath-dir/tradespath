Apprenticeships in Prince Edward Island

Who runs it: Apprenticeship Training, Government of Prince Edward Island (Workforce, Advanced Learning and Population).

How Canadian apprenticeship works

In most provinces you first need a job with an employer in the trade. Then you and the employer register you with the provincial apprenticeship authority. You alternate paid on-the-job hours with technical training blocks, and many trades lead to a Red Seal endorsement.

How to start

  1. You generally must be 18 (with youth and mature-student exceptions), have a high school completion certificate or equivalent, and work in a designated trade with a suitable employer.
  2. Complete the Apprenticeship Application and Agreement. You fill in your section and your employer fills in theirs, and you both sign the training agreement. Signatures must be witnessed. Submit it to the Apprenticeship office.
  3. Register early for in-school training. Each level usually lasts 5 to 10 weeks, and EI-eligible apprentices may get help with costs.
