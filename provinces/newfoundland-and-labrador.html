Apprenticeships in Newfoundland and Labrador

Who runs it: The Apprenticeship and Trades Certification Division (ATCD), Government of Newfoundland and Labrador.

How Canadian apprenticeship works

In most provinces you first need a job with an employer in the trade. Then you and the employer register you with the provincial apprenticeship authority. You alternate paid on-the-job hours with technical training blocks, and many trades lead to a Red Seal endorsement.

How to start

  1. Get a job in the trade with an employer who has a certified journeyperson to supervise your training.
  2. Fill out the Apprenticeship Application and the in-province Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). You and your employer both sign it.
  3. Include your official pre-employment or high school transcript.
  4. Submit it by email, mail or in person to the nearest ATCD office. Once you're approved, you get a registration card and an official logbook.
